\# Summary: MSTAR Benchmark \& Evaluation Protocols



\## Key Takeaways

\- \*\*Standard Operating Conditions (SOC):\*\* Evaluates performance using standard depression angle splits:

&#x20; - \*\*Training Set:\*\* $17^\\circ$ depression angle data.

&#x20; - \*\*Testing Set:\*\* $15^\\circ$ depression angle data.

\- \*\*Generalization:\*\* Testing on the $17^\\circ \\rightarrow 15^\\circ$ split verifies that models do not overfit to specific aspect/depression geometries.

\- \*\*Phase Sensitivity:\*\* Multi-look averaging often destroys complex phase distributions; benchmark performance depends heavily on processing single-look complex (SLC) inputs directly.
